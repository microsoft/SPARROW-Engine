## Reading this catalogue

Models are grouped by **domain** (camera trap, acoustics, overhead, marine imagery, general) and **task** (detector, classifier, encoder, cascade). Each row carries:

- **Status** — active published/hosted, active link-only/rights-pending, or candidate (not published). A candidate row never implies a current zoo download.
- **Display name / ID** — human-readable label and the stable `id` the engine and `download_models.sh` resolve models by.
- **Family** — model lineage (e.g. MegaDetector, SpeciesNet, DeepFaune, AddaxAI, DCLDE-orca).
- **Format / version** — artifact type (`onnx` desktop, `tflite` mobile, `cascade` descriptor, `ensemble` desktop recording-level package) with version / flavor. Ensembles contain multiple ONNX, DSP, and label assets rather than one weight file.
- **Behavior** — what the model emits: a detector that gates on a class (e.g. animal) versus one that outputs species directly, a classifier, an encoder, or a cascade pipeline.
- **Geography / locality** — global, foundational, or the regions / locality a regional model targets.
- **Developer / owner** — the party that built the model and, where different, the data owner.
- **AI4G relationship** — first-party (built by the Microsoft AI for Good Lab (AI4G)) versus third-party (external model onboarded into the zoo).

## Licensing at a glance

- **Hosting**: **Hosted** means redistribution is supported by the recorded grant. **Hosted-restricted** preserves conditions such as NonCommercial, academic-use, ShareAlike, or custom terms. **Link-only** directs users to the original owner. **Pending-rights** disables hosting while permission is clarified.
- **Commercial use**: **allowed**, **non-commercial**, or **unverified** reflects `commercial_use_status`. The legacy `commercial_use` Boolean is true only for **allowed**; false does not distinguish a prohibition from missing evidence.
- **Weight rights and framework licences are separate**. A framework's GPL/AGPL or other licence does not establish permission to redistribute or convert another party's trained checkpoint. Consult the per-model rights record and applicable framework notices.
- Consult each package's `LICENSE.md`, `ATTRIBUTION.md`, `MODEL_CARD.md`, `CONVERSION.md`, and `SOURCE.md` for the evidence, conversion basis, and restrictions. Recorded status is not legal advice.
